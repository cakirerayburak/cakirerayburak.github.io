Recognition & research

Registered patent / Co-inventor / 2026 / Application no. 2024/018955

Yapay Zekâ Kontrollü Dinamik Antenli Modem ile Sinyal ve Kapsama Optimizasyonu Sistemi ve Yöntemi

An AI-controlled dynamic-antenna modem that continuously optimizes antenna direction, signal strength, and wireless coverage based on environmental conditions and device demand.

  • Dual antenna system: 2.4 GHz wide-beam + 5 GHz patch
  • Servo-controlled steering: 0-180°
  • AI-based optimization: predictive model + clustering
  • Feedback-driven adjustment, energy-aware operation
Technical details

Purpose. Manage signal strength and coverage dynamically as environmental conditions and device demand change, rather than relying on fixed antennas that radiate equally in every direction.

Dual-antenna approach. A 2.4 GHz wide-beam antenna covers short ranges broadly; a 5 GHz patch antenna concentrates the signal for longer, focused links.

Servo control. The narrow-beam patch antenna is steered mechanically by a servo motor through 0-180°.

AI and feedback. An AI processor analyzes device feedback (signal level, stability, throughput) and usage patterns, then re-steers antennas and adjusts signal intensity.

Energy efficiency. Signal power scales with actual usage, aiming for lower consumption at idle.

The wide-beam 2.4 GHz antenna provides broad short-range coverage; the patch antenna remains at θ = 90°.
